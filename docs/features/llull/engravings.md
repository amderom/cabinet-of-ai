## Found while checking: the 1517 Lyon edition (all four figures)

*Illuminati sacre pagine professoris amplissimi magistri Raymundi Lull. Ars magna, generalis et ultima*, ed. Bernardus de Lavinheta, Lyon, Jacques Maréchal for Simon Vincent, 1517. Copy of the Getty Research Institute, scanned by the Internet Archive: [illuminatisacrep00llul](https://archive.org/details/illuminatisacrep00llul). The Public Domain Image Archive lists an image from this scan as **"Underlying rights: Public Domain Worldwide · Digital rights: No additional rights"** (confirmed by email with the source, it says). Hand-coloured woodcuts (green, red, ochre), with a reader's handwritten notes in the margins.
